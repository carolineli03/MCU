ABOUT THOSE STORY DATES.
Marvel has officially dated the timeline up to roughly Endgame — that's where the Iron Man-is-set-in-2010 placement comes from, which surprises people. Anything after the Blip is reconstructed from on-screen clues and the odd interview, so treat late-Phase-Five placements as best-guess rather than gospel. A handful of titles sit in other universes entirely and have no fixed MCU date at all.

ONE RECASTING NOTE.
A few roles changed hands: Bruce Banner, War Machine and Thaddeus Ross were all recast between films, and Robert Downey Jr. returns in Doomsday as Doctor Doom rather than Tony Stark. Marvel has not explained that last one, and probably won’t until December.
